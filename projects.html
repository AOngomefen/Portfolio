Global CO2 Emissions Analysis

"This project analyzes global CO2 emissions from 2000 - 2014 to identify trends, high-emitting countries, and differences between developed and developing nations. Using visualizations such as line and bar plots, the analysis highlights a steady global increase in emissions and reveals that a small group of wealthy, industrialized countries contribute disproportionately to global output. The results emphasize the role of economic development in shaping national emission patterns."
